## What English Prep is

English Prep is an independent revision companion for ZIMSEC English Language
4005 candidates. It is not an official ZIMSEC product.

## Information we process

Depending on which features the developer enables, the app may process:

- account credentials and authentication identifiers supplied through Supabase;
- display name and email address for account access and support;
- locally stored lessons, progress, bookmarks, accessibility preferences and
  scanned-question history;
- operational events such as feature use, app version, success/failure level and
  limited numeric metadata needed for reliability monitoring;
- learner questions, optional document images and coach prompts when the learner
  explicitly uses online coach or question-scanner features;
- device and technical information supplied by the operating system or hosting
  provider for security, diagnostics and service operation.

We do not intentionally request contacts, precise location, SMS, call logs or
advertising identifiers. The current Android app requests Internet and camera
access; camera content is used only when the learner starts a scan or attaches a
document.

## Why we process it

We use information to:

1. authenticate learners and keep accounts secure;
2. save progress and provide revision features;
3. answer an explicitly submitted coach or scanner request;
4. operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the service;
5. respond to support, deletion and legal requests.

The developer must document the applicable legal basis for each purpose with a
privacy adviser. For learners under 18, obtain and retain the required guardian
consent before processing personal information.

## Sharing and service providers

Account and application data may be processed by the configured Supabase
project. If online AI features are enabled, the submitted prompt or image may
be sent to the specifically configured AI provider shown in the product’s
current provider list. The developer must keep that list current and must not
enable a provider without reviewing its contract, location, retention and
training terms.

We do not sell learner information and do not use it for third-party
interest-based advertising.

## Retention

Local progress remains on the device until the learner clears app data or
uninstalls the app. Server-side account, profile, event and content retention
must be set in the production retention schedule and deleted when no longer
needed, subject to documented legal obligations.

## Security

Secrets are kept in server-side secret storage, not in the mobile bundle.
Access is restricted by authentication and database policies. No internet
service can guarantee absolute security; suspected incidents should be
reported to `[privacy email]`.

## Children and guardians

English Prep may be used by school-age learners. The developer must use an
age-appropriate notice, obtain written guardian consent where required, collect
only necessary information, avoid behavioural advertising, and provide a
guardian contact and deletion route.

## Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information by using
the public deletion URL or contacting `[privacy email]`. Account deletion must
remove the account and associated personal data except for narrowly documented
legal retention.

## Changes and complaints

We will publish material policy changes at this URL and update the effective
date. Contact `[privacy contact]` first for concerns, and contact the relevant
data-protection authority where appropriate.
