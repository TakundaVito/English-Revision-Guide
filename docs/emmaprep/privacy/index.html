English Prep privacy policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

This policy is a release candidate and must be reviewed by the developer's privacy adviser before it is linked from Google Play.

English Prep is an independent revision companion for ZIMSEC English Language 4005 candidates. It is not an official ZIMSEC product.

Information we process

Depending on enabled features, English Prep may process a display name, email address, authentication identifier, learning progress, bookmarks, accessibility preferences, operational events, learner prompts, and optional document images submitted to the coach or question scanner.

Progress and preferences may remain locally on the device. Account and service data may be processed by the configured Supabase project. If online AI features are enabled, an explicitly submitted prompt or image may be sent to the configured AI provider described to the learner before submission.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate learners, save revision progress, answer requested coach or scanner questions, secure and troubleshoot the service, and respond to support or deletion requests. We do not sell learner information or use it for third-party interest-based advertising.

Children and guardians

English Prep may be used by school-age learners. The developer obtains guardian consent where required, limits collection to what is necessary, and provides a deletion route. Guardians can contact takunda@vito.co.zw.

Retention and deletion

We retain information only for the documented service, security and legal purposes. To request account and associated-data deletion, visit the account-deletion page or email takunda@vito.co.zw. We verify the request, delete the account and associated personal data, and explain any narrowly required legal retention.

Security and contact

Secrets are kept in server-side secret storage and not in the mobile bundle. Access is restricted by authentication and database policies. For privacy questions or suspected incidents, contact takunda@vito.co.zw.

Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
