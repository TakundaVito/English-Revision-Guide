Delete your English Prep account

This page describes the request process. The developer must verify the production deletion workflow before this page is submitted to Google Play.

To request deletion of your English Prep account and associated personal data, email takunda@vito.co.zw from the account email address. If a guardian is making the request, say so in the message and do not include a password or one-time code.

What happens next

  1. We verify that the requester controls the account or is its authorised guardian.
  2. We disable access while the request is processed.
  3. We delete the Supabase account, learner profile and associated personal data.
  4. We remove or anonymise operational records that still reference the account.
  5. We send a completion notice to the verified contact.

Local progress may remain on a device until the app is uninstalled or its storage is cleared. We will explain any narrowly required legal retention. For questions, contact takunda@vito.co.zw.
