## Full description

English Prep is an independent revision companion for ZIMSEC English Language
4005 candidates.

Study Paper 1 and Paper 2 skills through short lessons, worked examples,
practice questions and progress tracking. Revise comprehension, inference,
summary, composition, functional writing, grammar, register, editing and
argument with explanations designed for focused study.

The app is designed to work offline for bundled lessons and practice. Optional
online features can provide refreshed curriculum content, an AI study coach and
question scanning when those services are enabled by the app owner.

English Prep is not an official ZIMSEC product and does not replace a teacher,
school or the official examination materials.
